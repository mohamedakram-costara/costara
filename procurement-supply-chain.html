Procurement & Supply Chain Cost Control

The lowest quotation is not automatically the lowest operational cost. Supplier quality, specification, yield, delivery reliability and consumption all affect the final cost.

Separate price from consumption

A price increase may be visible, while excess usage can be the larger hidden variance. Analyze both before calling more suppliers.

Compare total usable value

Compare specification, pack size, yield, damage, rejection, substitutions and lead time together with unit price.

Diversify intelligently

Multiple quotations do not guarantee supply resilience if all suppliers depend on the same manufacturer, importer or origin.

Costara principle

Measure the process, identify the controllable loss, calculate the financial impact, and convert it into an action plan.
