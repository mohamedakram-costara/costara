COSTARA FORMULA LIBRARY

Understand the numbers behind the movement.

Food Cost % is the result. The formulas below identify where that result was created.

01
CONSUMPTION CONTROL

Actual Consumption

Opening Stock + Purchases − Closing Stock

Measures the actual value of food consumed during the selected control period.

02
FOOD COST CONTROL

Food Cost Percentage

Actual Food Consumption ÷ Revenue × 100

Converts food consumption into a percentage of the revenue generated during the same period.

03
OPERATIONAL CONTROL

Standard Consumption

Operational Output × Standard Cost per Unit

Calculates what the operation should have consumed for the actual number of meals, portions or units produced.

04
VARIANCE CONTROL

Consumption Variance

Actual Consumption − Standard Consumption

Identifies the financial difference between what was actually consumed and what should have been consumed.

COSTARA CONTROL LOGIC

Measure the movement before measuring the percentage.

A Food Cost % tells management what happened. COSTARA is designed to help identify where, when and why it happened.
