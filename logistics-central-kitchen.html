Catering Logistics & Central Kitchen Cost

The cheapest kitchen is not always the kitchen with the lowest production cost. It is the kitchen that delivers the required meal safely, on time, at the lowest total cost.

Measure delivered-meal cost

Add distribution, loading, route, vehicle, manpower and re-delivery costs to production economics.

Use capacity as a network

Before buying new equipment, check whether unused productive capacity already exists in another kitchen.

Balance profit, not workload

Equal production allocation across kitchens can increase total cost. Allocate the next unit of demand where total safe delivered cost is lowest.

Costara principle

Measure the process, identify the controllable loss, calculate the financial impact, and convert it into an action plan.
