Daily Production Plan: Same Recipe, Same Cost? Wrong.

Before you cook today, count what yesterday safely left behind.

  • Start with confirmed demand minus safe usable balance plus approved buffer.
  • Do not assume recipe × headcount is the real production need.
  • Use conservative examples: even AED 0.10 per meal can create AED 30,000/month at 10,000 meals/day.
