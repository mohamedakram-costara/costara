Menu & Recipe Costing for Catering and Restaurants

A recipe is not financially controlled until ingredient price, usable yield, portion size and actual production behavior are connected.

Cost the usable quantity

Purchase price per kg can mislead when trimming and yield differ. Calculate the cost of the usable ingredient, not only the invoice price.

Protect portion consistency

Standard portions protect cost and guest consistency. Under-portioning is not a true saving; over-portioning is not hospitality when it destroys margin.

Engineer the menu

Review contribution margin, popularity, ingredient volatility, production complexity and operational capacity—not only food-cost percentage.

Costara principle

Measure the process, identify the controllable loss, calculate the financial impact, and convert it into an action plan.
