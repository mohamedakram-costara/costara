HACCP, CCP & Profit Protection

HACCP protects food safety first. Strong implementation also protects the money already invested in receiving, storage, production and service.

Prevent expensive failure

Temperature, receiving, storage, cooking, cooling and holding controls reduce spoilage, rejected batches and unnecessary rework.

Protect yield and consistency

Correct process controls help prevent overcooking, repeated cooking, contamination and product losses.

Use records financially

Repeated CCP deviations can reveal operational loss patterns. The same records that protect food safety can also help identify avoidable cost.

Costara principle

Measure the process, identify the controllable loss, calculate the financial impact, and convert it into an action plan.
