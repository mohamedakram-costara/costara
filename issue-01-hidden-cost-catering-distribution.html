The Hidden Cost of Catering Distribution

Your food cost may be under control — but is your delivery model eating your profit?

  • Measure cost per delivered meal, not only food cost.
  • Review routes, vehicle utilization, manpower, waiting time and failed deliveries.
  • Small route improvements can protect monthly net profit.
