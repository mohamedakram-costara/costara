Waste-to-Value: Where Profit Becomes Garbage

Do not start with the bin. Trace where the value was lost.

  • Separate preventable waste, yield loss, surplus, used oil, packaging and disposal.
  • Measure waste cost per meal and annualize the opportunity.
  • Turn root-cause analysis into action, not just waste recording.
