Inventory Control & Catering Audit

Inventory variance is not just a store problem. It can begin in purchasing, receiving, issuing, production, transfers, counting or system setup.

Reconcile the movement

Expected closing stock should reflect opening stock, purchases, transfers, issues and authorized adjustments before comparing it with physical stock.

Trace the mismatch

Investigate unit-of-measure errors, unposted GRNs, incorrect issues, transfer timing, duplicate postings, negative stock, counting mistakes and unauthorized consumption.

Turn variance into action

Rank exceptions by AED impact and frequency. Fix repeat causes first instead of spending time on immaterial differences.

Costara principle

Measure the process, identify the controllable loss, calculate the financial impact, and convert it into an action plan.
