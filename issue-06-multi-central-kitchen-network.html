Do Not Run Four Kitchens. Run One Production Network.

The cheapest kitchen is the one that delivers safely, on time, at the lowest total cost.

  • Plan by demand, capacity, equipment, specialization and logistics.
  • Compare cost per delivered meal across kitchens.
  • Balance profit, not only workload.
