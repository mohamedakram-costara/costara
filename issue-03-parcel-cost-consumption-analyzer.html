Parcel Cost Is High. Stop Calling More Suppliers.

Lowest unit price is not always the lowest delivered-meal cost.

  • Break variance into price, usage, quality, specification, process and supply risk.
  • Track pieces consumed per meal and lid/container mismatches.
  • Fix operational leakage before negotiating only a few fils.
